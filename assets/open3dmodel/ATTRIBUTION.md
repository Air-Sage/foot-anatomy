# AnatomyTOOL Open3DModel Lower Limb

This demo uses `lower-limb.glb` from AnatomyTOOL Open3DModel.

- Source page: https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel-create
- Download source: https://caskanatomy.info/open3dmodelfiles/lower-limb/lower-limb-glb.zip
- License stated by AnatomyTOOL Open3DModel: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

The model is used here as an educational, interactive anatomy visualization asset.

## Viewer rendering adjustments (2026-09-15)

The original GLB geometry and embedded textures are unchanged. The viewer retains
authored color and normal maps, adjusts material roughness and reflectance, and
adds environment illumination and screen-space contact shading. Teaching-region
overlay meshes are excluded from anatomical tissue layers.
